# Current illustration sources

The current square-context and city-evening SVG graphics are original generic concepts, not photographs or measured maps. They do not trace the St.George horse/rider/dragon sculpture, ornamental column or surrounding facades. Their captions, alt text and social metadata disclose their conceptual role in all three languages; they are not included as documentary TouristAttraction images.

The previous two JPEG photographs were removed because evidence for their intended depicted-work use remained unresolved. Their photographer licences were separately documented; removal is not a finding that the photography was unlicensed or illegal.
